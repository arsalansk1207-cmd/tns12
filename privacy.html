Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

TNS Arm Wrestling ("TNS", "we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information: username, email, city, weight class, pull hand, and password (stored locally in your browser).
  • Content you create: posts, comments, votes, and bookmarks.
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used (no third-party analytics currently deployed).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the TNS community platform.
  • To communicate event registrations and club updates.
  • To maintain leaderboard rankings and XP progression.

3. Data Storage

All community data is currently stored in your browser's localStorage. No data is transmitted to external servers. Your data stays on your device.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except as required by law.

5. Children

TNS Arm Wrestling is not directed at children under 13. Users must confirm they are 13 or older during registration.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us via @tnsarmwrestling on Instagram or visit us at NIBM, Pune.
