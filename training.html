Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY
ASKED

Is this program online or offline?
Our program is 100% online, making it accessible to anyone, anywhere in the world. All you need is an internet connection to access training materials and live sessions.
Do I need any special equipment to start?
You'll need basic equipment like dumbbells, resistance bands, and a training table. For beginners, we provide alternative exercises that can be done with minimal or no equipment. As you progress, we'll guide you on additional gear.
Will I receive a certificate after completing the program?
Yes! Upon successful completion of the Trainer or Referee programs, you'll receive an official TNS Armwrestling certification. For all other programs, you'll receive a certificate of completion to add to your portfolio.
What languages are the training materials available in?
Our training materials and live sessions are available in both English and Hindi. We're working on adding more language options in the future to make our programs accessible to a wider audience.
How much time should I dedicate each week?
We recommend at least 3–5 hours per week to see significant progress. Our programs are designed to be flexible, letting you learn at your own schedule — but consistency is key.
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Absolutely. Many athletes start at the Beginner level and work their way up. You can upgrade at any time — just reach out to us on WhatsApp and we'll handle the transition smoothly.
