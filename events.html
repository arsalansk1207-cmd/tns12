Got Questions?

EVENTS
FAQ

How do I register for an event?
Click any "Register Now" button on this page to fill out the registration form. You can also reach us directly on WhatsApp at +91 73878 78771. We'll confirm your slot and share event details.
What weight categories are available?
We run categories for men and women across multiple weight classes: Under 60kg, 60–70kg, 70–80kg, 80–90kg, 90–100kg, and 100kg+. Exact categories may vary per event — check the specific event for details.
Can beginners compete? Do I need experience?
Absolutely yes! TNS events are open to everyone — from first-timers to experienced competitors. We have beginner-friendly divisions and our referees walk new competitors through the rules before their match.
What are the entry fees and rules?
Entry fees vary per event (₹299–₹799 per category). All events follow official WAF (World Armwrestling Federation) rules. A copy of the rules is shared upon registration. College Battle is free for students.
What should I bring on event day?
Bring a valid ID, confirmation of registration, and comfortable sportswear. Wrist wraps are allowed. No rings or metal jewellery during matches. Arrive 30 minutes before your category starts for weigh-in.
Can I compete in multiple weight categories?
You can only compete in your official weight category, which is verified at weigh-in on event day. If you fall within the range of two categories, you may request to compete in the heavier one at the organiser's discretion.
