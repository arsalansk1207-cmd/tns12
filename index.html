Who We Are

THE STORY
BEHIND THE FIST

2017
Est. Pune

Welcome to TNS Arm Wrestling — Pune's first and premier arm wrestling club. Since 2017, when our founder Aavez & friends discovered the sport, TNS has grown from a local passion into a movement that's put Pune on India's arm wrestling map.

In 2022, we officially launched TNS as a brand — community, competition, and coaching all in one place. Proudly partnered with Decathlon, India's largest sports retailer.
